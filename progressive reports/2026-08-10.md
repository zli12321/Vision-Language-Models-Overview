# Progressive Research Report — August 10, 2026

> **Scope**: New VLMs, world models, benchmarks, training methods, applications, and challenges papers **not already listed** in the main `README.md` or in the previous progressive report (`2026-07-22.md`). Covers **after July 22, 2026** (≈3 weeks). This cycle's dominant theme: **evaluation stops asking whether a model sees**. The strongest new benchmarks ask instead whether it can *act through a body*, *hold a scene in memory*, and *react* rather than merely look right — and whether the thing doing the judging can itself be trusted.

### Highlights

- **Two trillion-scale multimodal models within three weeks, both Chinese.** Qwen3.8-Max (2.4T total, 95B active, 08/03) landed weeks after Kimi K3 (2.8T, 07/17), and reports at **#2 on Vision Arena**. Note a **conflict in public reporting** about Kimi K3's modality: Moonshot's model card describes native vision via a 401M MoonViT-V2 encoder, while several comparison write-ups list it as text-only. This survey's §1 entry follows the model card; treat third-party comparison tables with care.
- **Diffusion reaches the model families.** DiffusionGemma is the first diffusion-based release from a top-tier lab in the Gemma line, following LLaDA2.0-Uni and Mamoda2.5 from the open-weight side. The AR-vs-diffusion question is no longer confined to unified generation models.
- **Unified models reach 3D.** Hunyuan3D-Buffalo 1.0 carries the understand-and-generate recipe into 3D generation, understanding, and editing — a modality the Era-3b wave has largely skipped in favour of image, video, and audio.

## 7. Key Trends Observed

1. **Evaluation moved from "can it see" to "can it act and remember".** HumanCLAW asks whether a VLM can act through a body; GST-Bench and ChronoVision ask whether it can hold a scene across time; WorldExam asks whether a world model *reacts* rather than merely *looks* right. Three independent groups converged on the same critique in three weeks.
2. **Judging is becoming its own research problem.** OSReward standardizes reward models for computer-use agents, ConfBench evaluates calibration rather than accuracy, and TruthLens replaces the external judge with self-evaluation. As agentic trajectories get longer, verifying them is harder than producing them.
3. **Attribution is replacing aggregate scores.** HumanCLAW separates the model's decision from motor control so a failure can be traced to perception, planning, or actuation; ChronoVision attributes the passive-perception gap to a missing reconstructable latent state. Benchmarks are being designed to explain failures, not just rank systems.
4. **The frontier is invisible to paper feeds.** Qwen3.8-Max, the cycle's one trillion-scale multimodal launch, arrived as a blog post and a model card with no arXiv preprint. Any survey that tracks only arXiv now systematically lags the frontier by weeks.
5. **Hub popularity is a trap for recency.** The most-downloaded "new" multimodal weights this cycle were quantized re-uploads of Gemma 4 and a February release of MiniCPM-o 4.5 that merely received an update. Genuinely new original weights published in the window were almost entirely LoRAs and personal fine-tunes.
